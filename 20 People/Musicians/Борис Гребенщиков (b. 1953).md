	- Part of:
	- [[Russian Rock (≈1980s)]]
- Biography:
	- Russian singer, songwriter, and guitarist; founder and leader of the band Аквариум (formed in 1972 in Leningrad).
	- One of the central figures of Soviet and Russian rock, often called a founding father of the Leningrad rock scene.
	- Spent years in the late-Soviet underground, then reached a mass audience during perestroika in the 1980s.
- Core traits:
	- Word-centered songwriting:
		- His songs lean on poetry, imagery, and allusion; the voice and text usually carry more weight than instrumental display.
	- Eclectic borrowing:
		- Freely blends Western rock, folk, reggae, and Eastern and spiritual imagery into a personal style.
	- Bard and rock bridge:
		- Connects the Russian tradition of poetic author-song with Western rock instrumentation and sound.
	- Listening focus:
		- Follow the lyrics and their imagery first; the music often serves the text.
		- Notice how simple chord patterns and a calm voice build meaning through atmosphere rather than power.
