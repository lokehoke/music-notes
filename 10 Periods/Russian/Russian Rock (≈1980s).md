- Key ideas:
	- Rock built around words: lyrics, poetry, and meaning usually matter more than instrumental virtuosity
	- Grows out of the late-Soviet underground (Leningrad, Moscow), unofficial at first, then reaching a mass audience during perestroika in the 1980s
	- Bridges the Russian bard and poetic author-song tradition with Western rock forms and attitude
